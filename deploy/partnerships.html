Partnerships

Partnership with YBA is in kind: giving what you’re able to, in a way that directly benefits the next generation of BAs.

That might be practical support like covering website hosting, or it might be opening doors young BAs wouldn’t otherwise have.

Some examples of what our partners have contributed:

  • Covering website hosting and costs like banners, keeping YBA running behind the scenes
  • A seat on a major conference’s advisory board, giving YBA a genuine voice in shaping the industry’s direction
  • A stand or session slot at a conference, putting YBA and its members directly in front of the wider industry
  • Conference tickets, opening the door to events young BAs couldn’t otherwise afford or access
  • Hosting lightning talks for first-time speakers, giving people their first experience of presenting to an audience
  • Book review opportunities, giving early-career BAs a chance to read, review and be published or credited before they’d normally get that kind of visibility
  • Books, sent directly to members and Ambassadors to support their learning

If your organisation has something to offer, whether that’s books, event access, review opportunities, practical support or something else entirely, we’d love to hear from you.

Get in touch and we’ll have a chat about how we can work together.

Our partners

Our partners share something in common: they care about where the profession is heading. Partnering with YBA is a way to invest directly in that next generation and to be visible to an engaged, early-career audience.

  • AssistKD

    AssistKD is a UK training and consultancy provider specialising in Business Analysis and Service Design, running professional qualifications, apprenticeships, and courses. As our main partner, AssistKD sponsors YBA’s work across the board and has been instrumental in helping us build the group from the ground up.

    AssistKD

  • IRM UK

    IRM UK is a long-running organiser of premium conferences and training across business analysis, enterprise architecture, and business change. Their support centres on the BA Europe conference, where they give YBA a seat on the advisory board, a stand, and a lunchtime session slot, giving young BAs a genuine voice in helping shape the conference itself.

    IRM UK

  • BCS

    BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT, is the UK’s professional body for IT, working to advance the practice and education of computing for public benefit. BCS gives YBA book review opportunities, so the industry can hear directly from young BAs’ perspectives, and provides books as giveaways for our members and Ambassadors.

    BCS

  • BA Life

    BA Life runs the world’s largest virtual conference built by and for business analysts, bringing the BA community together for a full day of learning and connection. Every year, BA Life gives YBA a session and a ticket, helping our community get in front of a wider audience.

    BA Life

  • IIBA UK

    IIBA UK is the UK chapter of the International Institute of Business Analysis, an independent, entirely volunteer-run body dedicated to helping BAs connect, share knowledge, and grow their careers. We run collaborative events together with IIBA UK, bringing our communities closer and giving young BAs more chances to connect with the wider profession.

    IIBA UK
